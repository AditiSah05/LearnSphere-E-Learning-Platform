Last updated: August 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you sign up, enroll in a course, or contact us, we collect the information you provide directly — name, email address, and any message content. Course activity (enrollments, progress, wishlist, cart) is stored to run the site's features.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to create and manage your account, track course progress, process enrollments, respond to inquiries, and improve the platform. We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

3. Cookies & Local Storage

LearnSphere uses browser local storage to remember your cart, wishlist, enrolled courses, and reviews on this device. Clearing your browser data will remove this information.

4. Data Sharing

We may share information with service providers who help us operate the site (such as email delivery services), only as needed to provide our services, and never for their own marketing purposes.

5. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your information, but no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

6. Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data at any time by contacting us through the Contact page.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Continued use of LearnSphere after changes means you accept the revised policy.
